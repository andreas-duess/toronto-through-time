What the site is, and is not

The site is a history project made for learning and enjoyment. It is a model, not a survey. Many building years are best guesses, old maps were read by computer, and there are mistakes. How it was made explains what is known and what is guessed.

Do not rely on the site for anything that matters: buying or selling property, legal or planning questions, heritage decisions, finding your way, or planning a trip. The positions of buses, streetcars and bikes and the weather come from other services and can be late or wrong.
