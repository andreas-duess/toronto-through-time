✈ Aerial photographs

Aerial photography of the city, starting 1931

The city took photos of Toronto from a plane in 1931, 1939, 1954, 1965, 1978 and in most years since 1999. Turn this on to see the photo closest to the year you picked. The drawn map and the 3D buildings are hidden, because the photo shows the real ones. The line above tells you which year's photo you are looking at. It can be a few years away from the year on the slider.

◉ Archive photographs

Each camera pin is a spot where an old photo was taken, within two years of the year you picked. Click a pin to see the photos. The photos come from the City of Toronto Archives. A pin shows the place named in the photo's title, so it can be a little off.

◧ Data quality

For some buildings we know the year they were built. It is written down in a city record, a building permit or an article. For most buildings nobody wrote it down, so the year is our best guess, based on how old the area is. Turn this on to colour each building by how we know its year. A key shows up on the right. Grey means a guess.

The trees show only in today's year. Where they grow comes from a 2018 city study that used satellite photos. The single trees are drawn in to fill those areas, about 341,000 of them.

▬ Buses and streetcars

These are real TTC buses and streetcars, shown where they are right now. The positions can be up to 30 seconds old. Streetcars are red. Buses are dark blue. Click one to see its route.

⚲ Bike Share

Each orange bike marks a Bike Share Toronto station. Click one to see how many bikes and empty docks it has right now.

✈ Air traffic

Aircraft over Toronto right now, at their real height. Click one to see its flight number, type and height. The positions come from volunteers' radio receivers and are a few seconds old.

☁ Weather

Shows the weather in Toronto right now: rain or snow falling, duller light under cloud, and white ground when snow is lying. Turn it off for a clear day.
